# 04 — ChatGPT Generation Prompts

These prompts are for reconstruction backgrounds, prop sheets, and metaphorical editorial assets. Generated output must never be presented as an original historical document or portrait.

## Signature-style block

Paste this at the end of every prompt unless it is already included:

> Franklin channel visual identity: an old financial archive calmly dismantled and reconstructed by a modern analyst; restrained late-eighteenth-century engraving combined with clean contemporary editorial composition; warm ivory paper #E7E0D2, warm white #F4F0E8, deep ink #191919, charcoal #2B2B2B, banknote green #526B5A and muted gold #A88D57; muted red only as a tiny semantic warning; subtle 3–8% paper grain; soft directional light; one dominant object and no more than two supporting elements; understated, historically plausible, intelligent, human, never horror. No readable text, letters, numerals, watermarks, logos, borders, glossy 3D, neon, steampunk, heavy grunge, fake handwriting, or gibberish. Leave clean negative space for titles and citations to be added later in Resolve.

## Follow-up prompts for useful variants

After a good first image, ask:

- “Keep the exact composition and lighting. Remove all text-like markings and make the paper completely blank.”
- “Produce a clean plate with the foreground objects removed.”
- “Produce the foreground objects alone on transparent background.”
- “Extend the canvas to 3840×2160 without changing object proportions.”
- “Move the dominant object into the left third and preserve empty space on the right for citations.”
- “Reduce texture by half; keep only subtle paper grain.”
- “Make the banknote green and muted gold 20% less saturated.”
- “Preserve every object, but separate overlaps so each can be masked cleanly.”
- “Generate a second camera angle that clearly belongs to the same room and time of day.”
- “Remove any accidental letters, numbers, seals, crests, logos, signatures, or pseudo-writing.”

## Historical-integrity label

Every generated historical-looking frame should carry one of these small labels when context could confuse viewers:

- `VISUAL RECONSTRUCTION`
- `ILLUSTRATIVE OBJECTS`
- `MAP ANIMATION BASED ON PERIOD SOURCES`

Do not label stylized diagrams as archival records.
