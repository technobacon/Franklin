# 03 — Special Assets and Fusion Builds

These are the pieces worth building once and reusing. All “documents” below are modern explanatory graphics unless they are explicitly based on A01/A02/A03. Do not artificially age a generated page until viewers could mistake it for evidence.

## Reusable system kit

### F01 — Document focus rig

- Inputs: 4K scan, optional manually typed transcription, source line.
- Controls: crop, 4–8 second push, rectangular magnifier, single-line underline, edge vignette, dust removal.
- Styling: ivory highlight at 15% opacity; muted gold underline; no red circles.
- Deliverables: `F01_document-focus.comp`, `F01_source-footer.setting`.
- Use: every A01/A02/A03/A07 appearance.
- Rule: check the underline against the scan at 200% before export.

### F02 — Thirty-life marker grid

- Layout: 5 × 6 grid of identical small engraved oval markers.
- States: unknown grey, alive green, dead charcoal with a single muted-red rule, missing/uncertain gold outline.
- Animation: markers populate in 0.08-second intervals; state changes take 8–12 frames.
- Use: 0:11, 3:28, 9:22, final count.
- Never: replace markers with generated children's portraits.

### F03 — Rente viagère diagram

- Frame 1: INVESTOR gives LUMP SUM to FRENCH STATE.
- Frame 2: STATE sends ANNUAL PAYMENT while NAMED LIFE is alive.
- Frame 3: named-life marker dims; payment line stops; principal does not return.
- Visual: thin engraved arrows on warm paper, one coin stack, one name marker, no 3D coins.
- Use: 2:20–3:01 and simplified reprise at 11:13.

### F04 — Alive/payment binary

- Two cards only: `ALIVE — PAYMENT CONTINUES`; `DEAD — PAYMENT STOPS`.
- Type: Inter uppercase for system label; EB Garamond for the human name beneath.
- Red is permitted only on the “stops” rule, not across a child's name.
- Use: 0:50 and 11:00.

### F05 — Headcount board

- Morning: 27.
- Afternoon: 100+.
- Supporting line: `1 JULY 1784`.
- Animation: physical tally marks; numbers settle without bounce.
- Add note in the comp metadata: this is the representative's broader headcount, not the January 1782 cohort.

### F06 — Thirty-name index

- One verified transcription row per girl, numbered 01–30.
- Do not populate from OCR alone. Compare with the scan and research notes.
- Use only after name verification; otherwise use the scan.
- Final frame may remove numbering so every name is equal.

### F07 — Age strip

- Horizontal range: “under one” to “eight.”
- Position Pernette at nineteen months and Elisabeth-Pauline at three.
- Neutral marks only; no silhouettes of known children.
- Use: 1:40–2:05.

### F08 — Proof-of-life route map

- Base: A05 for Geneva close view; a separate clean engraved Europe base for long routes.
- Pins: Geneva, Paris, Bern, Lyon, Marseille, England.
- Route states: sent green; delayed gold; lost broken grey.
- Animation: 2–3 seconds per route; no airplane-style arcs.
- Use: payment setup, travel, and certificate section.

### F09 — Daughter/father pairing

- Left: girl's name; right: father's name.
- Source: A02 only.
- Connector: one muted-gold hairline.
- Build three verified examples rather than a speculative family tree.
- Use: 2:05–2:12 and selection section.

### F10 — Term card: rente viagère

- Historical serif term large; modern sans explanation small.
- Suggested copy: `a payment lasting as long as a named life`.
- Source footer: Cramer 1946, pp. 109–10.
- Duration: 5–7 seconds.

### F11 — Owner versus measuring life

- Two columns: `OWNS THE INCOME` / `KEEPS IT RUNNING`.
- Show that the same person need not occupy both roles.
- Keep gender/age neutral except the named-life marker can be child-height.

### F12 — Six-sister risk split

- One income bar divides into six equal threads.
- If one thread ends, five continue.
- This illustrates Spang's Diodati example; name Colombine only if the narration does.
- Do not draw portraits or claim equal inheritance details beyond the source.

### F13 — Genevan surname network

- Nodes: Pictet, Cramer, Sarasin, Diodati, Rilliet, de La Rive.
- Connections represent the selection pool/network, not necessarily direct kinship.
- Label the graphic `KNOWN GENEVA FAMILIES`, not `FAMILY TREE`.
- Use A02/Cramer to verify any explicit relationship before drawing a line.

### F14 — Selection comparison

- Column A: girls — expected longevity, less military exposure.
- Column B: rarer boys — stay-in-Geneva incentive, avoid dangerous occupation.
- Source footer: Cramer 1946, pp. 115–16.
- Keep the language as reported historical logic, not modern biological fact.

### F15 — Stay/leave city mini-map

- City gate icon, inward green arrow, outward grey arrow.
- Small pension token sits beside “remain in Geneva.”
- Use 8–10 seconds; this is explanatory, not a complete military history.

### F16 — Signature absence card

- Three blank signature lines.
- Caption: `UNABLE TO READ OR WRITE — SIGNATURE NOT REQUIRED`.
- Source footer: Spang 2015, pp. 27–28.
- Do not animate fake signatures erasing themselves.

### F17 — Surname/change card

- Top line: birth name.
- Bottom line: married name or new address.
- A thin rule connects them; original name never disappears.
- Use for Anne Audéoud/Gabriel Cramer and later for Bertrand/Wickham and Rolaz/de Boisy.

### F18 — Proof-of-life field explainer

- Modern card with fields: name, current place, date seen, witness, seal.
- Label clearly: `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT AN ORIGINAL CERTIFICATE`.
- The structure may animate over a real certificate only if one is obtained and the match is accurate.
- Use at 6:40 and 7:52.

### F19 — Four half-years overdue

- Four half-year payment slips or four empty ledger cells.
- One letter route from England breaks before Geneva.
- Copy: `4 HALF-YEARS LATE`.
- Source: Cramer 1946, pp. 118–19.

### F20 — Six half-years overdue

- Six empty ledger cells.
- Marriage card is misaligned with bank record; it clicks into place after explanation.
- Copy: `6 HALF-YEARS LATE`.
- Source: Cramer 1946, pp. 118–19.

### F21 — Pernette name card

- Exact verified name and birth date from A01.
- Secondary line: `nineteen months old when listed`.
- Later state: `died aged eight`.
- Keep the page visible behind it; no portrait, silhouette, candle, heartbeat, or dramatic red.

### F22 — 212,197-livre hero number

- Number grows out of a narrow ledger column.
- On-screen qualifier: `annual annuity payments attached to her life — Cramer's archival reconstruction`.
- Use “more than 212,000 livres” in the large read; keep 212,197 in small exact type if verified.
- Do not convert to a modern currency unless a separate, defensible methodology is added.

### F23 — Ledger-line stop

- One horizontal payment line reaches the margin and stops.
- A small clerk's rule passes through the next expected payment cell.
- No blood-red wipe; muted red only on the stopped segment.

### F24 — Brief mortality cards

- `Colombine Diodati — died at 20`
- `Jeanne Pictet — died at 4`
- `later pool — 3 of 30 lost in year one`
- Verify exact wording and citations from Cramer p. 116.
- One card at a time, 3–4 seconds each.

### F25 — Elisabeth-Pauline life bar

- Range: 1778–1862.
- Event ticks: 1783, 1789, 1804, 1815, 1848, 1862.
- Labels should be minimal: American war ends; Bastille; Napoleon; Geneva joins Swiss Confederation; revolution of 1848; death.
- The bar stays green; event cards are grey/gold.
- Do not turn the sequence into a France-first montage.

### F26 — Thin biography folder

- Left pane: two confirmed dates and a few family-reference tabs.
- Right pane: intentionally empty warm paper.
- Dense annuity/ledger fragments sit behind the folder.
- Use to make the asymmetry visible without inventing a biography.

### F27 — Name-through-life cards

- Original surname remains on top.
- Married surname, city, or address arrives below.
- Three cards maximum at once.
- Final transition aligns the changing cards back with the fixed names on A01.

## Generated/reconstruction asset layers

### G01 — Empty reception-room reconstruction

Deliver as:

1. clean 16:9 room background;
2. doorway mask;
3. desk and list foreground;
4. separate window-light layer;
5. optional child-height shadow layer with no bodies.

A simple 2.5D push can carry 20 seconds by changing crop and doorway state.

### G02 — Anonymous arrival fragments

Create a six-panel prop sheet: plain period shoes, dress hems, adult coat hem, gloved hand, door handle, polished floor reflection. Keep all faces and full bodies absent.

### G04 — Blank name-slip stack

Thirty blank ivory slips, engraved edges, separated or on transparent background. Add verified names later in Resolve.

### G06 — Clean engraved Europe map

Late-18th-century inspired, geographically credible coastlines, no labels, no fantasy cartouche, no borders that imply a precise year. Add all place names and routes in Fusion.

### G08 — Annuity prop kit

Transparent PNG sheet: simple coin stack, folded payment slip, tied bundle, small institutional seal, blank oval name token. No text or national insignia.

### G12 — Generic Genevan townhouse

Historically plausible but explicitly reconstructed. Use as a family-home symbol, never as the documented house of a named girl.

### G13/G16 — Care and medical tabletop

Separate cutouts: ceramic bowl, folded linen, small wooden medical case, glass vial, non-gory period lancet. The medical items must be tagged “illustrative.”

### G18 — Empty portrait frames

Thirty small blank oval frames with subtle variation, no silhouettes. The emptiness visualizes absent likenesses without substituting people.

### G19 — Museum-plinth extension

Neutral dark-charcoal gallery background, ivory plaster dust and soft side light, built to extend A04 for a 2.5D move. Do not alter Anne's face or sculptural details.

### G22/G23 — Certificate and lost-letter props

Blank paper, folded envelope, wax seal, string, postal packet, and torn route line. No readable text. Add real names only as modern overlay and label the certificate illustrative.

### G24/G26 — Archive folders

Two period-neutral document folders: one nearly empty and one dense with anonymous ledger edges. No readable handwriting. Designed for the Pernette and Elisabeth sections.

## Source-footer master

Build one lower-third with:

- warm-white translucent plate, 88–92% opacity;
- 22–28 px Inter/IBM Plex Sans at 4K;
- source name, short title, year, page/object/shelf mark;
- 6–8 second minimum readable duration;
- optional tiny link icon, but no long URL;
- no “SOURCE:” label unless clarity requires it.

## Efficiency order

Build F01, F02, F03, F08, F18, F22, and F25 first. Together with A01–A05, they cover the core argument. G-assets are fallbacks and atmosphere, not prerequisites.
