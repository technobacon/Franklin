### Cramer page targets

- Printed pp. 109–10: mechanism and terminology.
- Printed pp. 115–16: selection logic, families, medical monitoring, Colombine, Jeanne, Pernette, and the 212,197-livre calculation.
- Printed pp. 118–19: Eléonore Bertrand and Jacqueline Rolaz proof-of-life/payment delays.

Good selection rules:

- Prefer a source institution's original download page over a repost.
- Prefer images whose date and location are within one generation of the story.
- A later historical photo can show a street or archive, but label it by date; do not silently pass it off as 1780s Geneva.
- A generic portrait may illustrate clothing, never a named girl.

## Suggested archive request

When contacting the Archives d’État de Genève or Bibliothèque de Genève, ask for:

1. A scan and shelf reference for any surviving proof-of-life certificate tied to Eléonore-Madeleine Bertrand or Jacqueline Rolaz.
2. Any contract or banker correspondence showing a married surname/address change.
3. Any document bearing an official witness signature or seal for a named measuring life.
4. Permission terms for commercial documentary use on YouTube.
5. Required credit line, preferred stable link, and whether cropping/animation is permitted.

One genuine proof-of-life certificate would be the most valuable additional asset in the entire production.
