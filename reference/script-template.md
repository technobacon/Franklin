## General visual style

Write two to four sentences covering palette, mood, asset mix, Franklin's role, and motion language. Default to restrained editorial collage: warm paper tones, black-and-white archival material, currency green as a controlled accent, official documents, clean charts, simple diagrams, and modest mascot reactions. Use motion to direct attention rather than keep the frame permanently busy.

## Factual drafting pass

Write the first complete draft for accuracy, causal logic, and spoken clarity. Do not perform ornamental voice polishing.

For every chapter, note:

- the chapter's one job;
- its load-bearing claims and ledger references;
- the necessary qualification;
- the handoff to the next idea, only if needed;
- the conceptual visual turn.

## Targeted voice revision

Make one focused revision of the diagnosed passages and necessary joins. Do not globally repolish the script.

If a further pass is needed, record why:

- new factual information;
- structural change;
- creator-requested direction;
- read-aloud substitution;
- other specific defect.

“Make it more human” is not a sufficient reason for another broad pass.

## Creator read-aloud

Highlight every line the creator instinctively paraphrases.

For each accepted substitution:

- preserve the spontaneous wording;
- verify that factual meaning and qualification remain intact;
- add it to `reference/voice-corpus.md` as **RS — Read-aloud substitution**;
- update the script.

## Final audit

### Accuracy

- [ ] Important claims appear in the ledger.
- [ ] Numbers have units, periods, and denominators.
- [ ] Current facts were checked at the stated cutoff.
- [ ] Interpretation is not presented as settled fact.
- [ ] No private employer or client information appears.

### Argument

- [ ] Every chapter has a distinct job.
- [ ] The causal links are explicit.
- [ ] The strongest competing explanation receives a fair hearing.
- [ ] The conclusion follows from the evidence.
- [ ] No chapter circles a point because research is missing.

### Voice

- [ ] Actual samples outranked broad style adjectives.
- [ ] Creator-supplied judgments were preserved, not invented.
- [ ] Sentence rhythm varies without becoming a new pattern.
- [ ] Actors and verbs dominate abstract framing.
- [ ] Useful plain repetition remains.
- [ ] Signposting and summaries have earned their space.
- [ ] Rhetorical devices do not form a house rhythm.
- [ ] The draft sounds plausible aloud.
- [ ] The diagnostic preceded one targeted revision.
- [ ] Read-aloud substitutions were captured.

### Production

- [ ] Visual cues are specific and feasible.
- [ ] Charts identify units, dates, and sources.
- [ ] Franklin supports rather than crowds the evidence.
- [ ] The title and thumbnail promise are fulfilled.
